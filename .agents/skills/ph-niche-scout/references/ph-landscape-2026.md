# Philippine Job Market & Modern Resume Landscape (2025–2026)

## Modern PH Resume Standards vs. Outdated Biodata

Top-performing 2025–2026 Philippine resumes targeting MNCs, local tech scale-ups, BPO leadership, and direct US/AU/EU remote employers follow international ATS-ready standards:

- **Include:**
  - Clean reverse-chronological structure (no multi-column tables that break ATS text parsing).
  - City & Region only (e.g., `Taguig City, Metro Manila` or `Cebu City, Central Visayas`) — never full street/house numbers.
  - Work setup & timezone readiness (e.g., `Hybrid (BGC) / Remote (US EST overlap)`).
  - Quantified impact bullets (`action verb + measurable business/operational result + tool/method`).
  - Concise top Professional Summary (3–4 lines, outcome-focused, zero clichés).
  - Relevant certifications (AWS, ISTQB, TESDA NC II/III, HIPAA, Xero/QuickBooks, COPC, Six Sigma) and live portfolio/GitHub slugs where applicable.
- **Strictly Exclude (Outdated PH Biodata):**
  - 2x2 or 1x1 ID photos (unless specifically requested by non-ATS traditional hospitality/aviation; never in Tech/BPO-VA gold standards).
  - Personal demographics: age, date of birth, height, weight, religion, civil/marital status, father/mother occupation.
  - Government ID numbers (TIN, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, UMID, PRC license exact number).
  - `"Objective: To obtain a challenging position..."` or `"Character References available upon request"` or `"I hereby certify that the above information is true and correct."`
